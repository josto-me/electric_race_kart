# Chain sizing

Tensile load on the drive chain at the short-time peak torque of the motor.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Pitch diameter of the motor sprocket d | 55 mm |
| Short-time peak torque of the motor M | 100 Nm |

$$F = \frac{M}{d/2} = \frac{100\ \text{Nm}}{0.055\ \text{m} \cdot 0.5} = 3636\ \text{N}$$

The tensile load on the chain is 3636 N (about 3.6 kN).
